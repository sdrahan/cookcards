# docs/01-vision.md

## What This Product Is
CookCards is a small web application that turns unstructured recipes
(text or photos) into clean, printable recipe cards.

## Who It Is For
- Individuals managing personal recipe collections
- Users who value simplicity and printability
- Non-technical users

## Problems It Solves
- Recipes scattered across formats
- Messy, inconsistent layouts
- Difficulty printing clean recipes

## What It Is Not
- A social platform
- A recipe discovery service
- A nutrition tracker
- A mobile app
- A content management system

This is a **personal utility**, not a platform.
